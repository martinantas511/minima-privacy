Delete your data

Minima Launcher and Together: Couple Widgets · 14 November 2026

Neither app has accounts. Your phone has a random identifier, and everything on the server belongs to a connection (the people you share widgets with).

Delete what you sent, in the app

  1. Open Together (or Minima → Settings → Minima Together).
  2. Open the connection, then tap Disconnect and delete.
  3. Confirm. Your photos, notes, voice notes, surprises, diary entries, favourites, places and your strokes on the current page are deleted from the server right away. When the last person leaves, the whole connection is deleted.

Drawings you made together on earlier pages stay with the others, because they belong to both of you.

Ask us to delete a whole connection

Write to martinantas511@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Tell us which app you use and, if you can, the name of the connection. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchases of Together+ are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as the law requires.
  • A record that this phone already used the free week, so it is not given twice.
  • Reports you sent about someone, until we have reviewed them.
  • Crash reports (anonymous) for 90 days at Firebase Crashlytics.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes everything from your phone.

Zmazanie údajov

Appky nemajú účty. Na serveri sú len veci, ktoré ste poslali do spojenia.

  1. Otvorte Together (alebo Minima → Nastavenia → Minima Spolu).
  2. Otvorte spojenie a ťuknite na Odpojiť a zmazať.
  3. Potvrďte. Vaše fotky, odkazy, hlasové odkazy, prekvapenia, záznamy v denníku, obľúbené, miesta a vaše ťahy na aktuálnom papieri sa hneď zmažú. Keď odíde posledný človek, zmaže sa celé spojenie.

Celé spojenie zmažeme aj na požiadanie: napíšte na martinantas511@gmail.com s predmetom „Zmazať údaje“. Vymažeme ho do 30 dní.
